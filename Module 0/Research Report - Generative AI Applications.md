## 1. AI Music Production
GenAI can turn text prompts and musical references into complete songs or segments. Additionally, users can specify the desired genre, mood, tempo, instruments, etc. to fine-tune the results to their imagination
- **Business Value**
	- The main business value is faster productive cycles. Ideas become a reality within a single click of a button* (with some typing/text-to-speech). Some are advertising it as lowering the entry barrier for independent musicians and smaller studios/companies. Additionally, this leads to a faster iterative process. making mixing and mastering a song a lot more convenient
- **Technical Challenges & Weak Points**
	- Generated music may not always follow the intended structure, sentiment, or rhythm. Additionally, some songs may sound very similar even though the prompts are distinct from each other. Finally, the AI-generated music will be subject to copyright laws if they are used to make money and are at risk of not sounding original to the public
- **Existing Implementations**
		- [Suno AI](https://suno.com) - Generates songs from text prompts with lyrics
## 2. AI-Powered Task Manager
A user can utilize AI to create, manage, and organize their tasks, notes documents, and reference materials. So the AI turns from a chatbot into a "second brain" (Obsidian's marketing slogan I think?)
- **Business Values**
	- Reduced time spent on reviewing and organizing notes. Additionally, streamlining the process of finding and quoting literature reviews and noticing contradictions between multiple notes in a single project/folder
- **Technical Challenges & Weak Points**
	- The main challenge is reliable integration with existing services that the users are likely to be already utilizing. The AI can also accidentally delete or remove important information from notes, so keeping a thorough backup of the existing notes adds a layer of "security maintenance" to a most likely complicated note-taking process. Finally, privacy and source validity is a real concern because AI can easily both mention a non-existing source and potentially open up another vulnerability by storing the data in its own memory
- **Existing Implementations**
	- [TickTick AI](https://help.ticktick.com/articles/7444685542580551680) - Integrated, but very simplistic. Runs only on tasks entered in TickTick
	- [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/brain) - Has ClickUp Brain but honestly feels very barebones, lacks depth and ease of use
	- [claude-obsidian](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-obsidian) - GitHub repo that utilizes AI in Obsidian to organize and control notes. Haven't tried it, don't wanna risk making my notes public
