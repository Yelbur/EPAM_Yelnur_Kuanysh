1. **Generative AI**
	- AI that generates new content (text, images, audio, video, code, etc.) after learning patterns from training data
2. **Large Language Model**
	- A Neural Network trained on large amounts of text. Aims to understand and generate answers in a comprehensible human language
3. **Transformer**
	- A Neural Network architecture that processes data to identify relationships between them. Foundational piece of modern LLMs
4. **Inference**
	- Process of using a trained AI model to generate predictions, responses, etc. from data
5. **Temperature**
	- Parameter in charge of an AI's randomness in outputs
6. **Prompt Engineering**
	- Method of properly preparing prompts for AI and iterating throughout the conversation to achieve a useful output
7. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**
	- Approach that combines information retrieval with GenAI. Does so by retrieving external data and providing it to a model to produce more accurate responses
8. **Orchestration**
	- Coordinating AI models to accomplish a collective task
9. **Artificial Neural Network**
	- Computational Model made up of interconnected nodes in layers that learn patters and take and output data until a final output deliverable
10. **Access Control**
	- Rules that determine which users or systems can view, modify, or delete particular data or invoke specific tools. It is especially important when an AI assistant can access private notes or work documents.
11. **Fine-Tuning**
	- Further training a pretrained model on a smaller, targeted dataset to adapt its behavior or performance to a particular task or domain.
12. **Token**
	- A unit of text processed by a language model. A token may be a word, part of a word, punctuation mark, or other text fragment; token counts affect context limits and usage costs.
13. **Prompt Injection**
	- An attack in which malicious or misleading instructions are placed in user input or retrieved content to manipulate an AI system into ignoring its intended instructions or misusing its tools.
